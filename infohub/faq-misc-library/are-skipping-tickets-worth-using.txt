Purely from a financial point of view, most skipping tickets are not worth using for most people. It is up to you how much you value your other goals, like log completion, and whether you think those other goals bear enough weight to make skipping tickets worth using for you.

Open this spreadsheet to see at how much you need to value your time in order to justify using certain skips, purely from a financial point of view: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1IuSFqS25HprQ_zDti_NMMoP5p693Y7Qz19NDKx6MjLo/edit?usp=sharing.

Costume skipping tickets are not included cause they have a lot of use until you get the full globetrotter outfit, and no use at all after that.

For overview how much of your profit will be eaten up by using skips, use this: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1w7jpuZMnkhXafTYNgcNjDUWwjVfl48mPr4ehE0eI57k/edit#gid=1645276893
